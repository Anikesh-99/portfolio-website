The point of this repo is not "here are some high-Sharpe formulas." It's the
selection methodology: how I searched a large space of signals on WorldQuant's
BRAIN platform, how I avoided fooling myself, and what actually survived a
robustness gate.

## The multiple-comparisons problem

Test hundreds of signals and some will look great by pure chance. Ranking by
in-sample Sharpe and presenting the top one would be exactly the wrong thing
to do, so selection ran through a gate instead of a max: a signal only counts
if it passed all of WorldQuant's platform checks and kept a Sharpe of at
least 1.0 on a different sub-universe of names than the one it was scored on.

![selection funnel](/projects/wq-funnel.svg)

## Reporting the whole distribution

Median in-sample Sharpe across all 259 alphas was 0.64 (max 2.10, min -1.66).
Most ideas were mediocre or worse, and saying so is the honest summary. I also
weighted convergence over outliers: a family of related signals that all
survive is worth more than one lucky formula.
