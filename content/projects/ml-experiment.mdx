## The problem

Threshold-based alerting either paged too often or missed real incidents.
Could a simple model do better than static thresholds — without the
operational cost of a full ML platform?

## What I built

A seasonal-decomposition detector that learns each metric's weekly rhythm and
alerts on deviations from the *residual*, not the raw value. No GPUs, no
training pipeline — it fits in a cron job.

## Results

- False-positive pages reduced by ~60% against the static-threshold baseline
- The whole detector is ~400 lines and one dependency
- Deliberately chose statistics over deep learning: easier to explain to the
  on-call engineer at 3am
