Risk questions on a trading desk are simple to ask and annoying to answer:
"what's my total DV01 across rates books?" means someone opens a risk report,
filters, and aggregates. This project puts a tool-calling agent in front of the
risk data so the question itself is the interface.

## How it works

The agent doesn't guess numbers. It picks from a set of typed tools
(portfolio Greeks, VaR, DV01 by book), calls them against live position data,
and composes the answer from what the tools return. The numbers in the answer
are always the numbers in the book.

![tool-calling architecture](/projects/risk-query-arch.svg)

## Why an eval harness

An answer that sounds right and an answer that is right are different things
in risk. The harness runs 50 labeled desk queries and scores routing accuracy,
answer rate, and latency, so tool-selection and routing regressions show up as
failing cases instead of wrong numbers reaching a user.
