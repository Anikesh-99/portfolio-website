## The problem

The on-call rotation had four browser tabs permanently open: metrics, logs,
deploys, and alerts — each in a different tool with a different login. Context
switching during incidents cost real minutes.

## What I built

A single dashboard aggregating all four sources over WebSockets, with an
incident view that automatically correlates deploy timestamps against error
spikes.

## Results

- Mean time-to-diagnosis during incidents dropped ~40%
- The four legacy tools were decommissioned within a quarter
- Adopted by three other teams without modification
