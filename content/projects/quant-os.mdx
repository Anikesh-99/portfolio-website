Quant research scatters across tools: papers in one tab, news in another,
backtests in notebooks. quant_os is my attempt at putting the loop in one
place: read, form a hypothesis, test it.

## Architecture

A React/TypeScript frontend talks to a Python backend; Docker Compose brings
the whole stack up with one command. The backend owns data access and the
backtest runner, the frontend owns reading and annotation workflows.

This one is a workbench rather than a polished product: it exists to make my
own research loop faster, and it earns its keep every time a paper idea goes
from PDF to backtest without leaving the app.
