Everyone publishes a tools list; almost nobody publishes the *removals*. Here
are both.

## Kept

- **A terminal-first workflow** — most of my day happens in a shell and an
  editor, and every tool that requires a mouse click has to earn it
- **A plain-text notes system** — grep beats every fancy search feature I've
  tried
- **CI that runs in under five minutes** — anything slower and people stop
  waiting for green

## Cut

- Three separate to-do apps, replaced by one text file
- A dashboard I looked at only when it was already too late to matter

The pattern in what survived: boring, fast, and inspectable.
